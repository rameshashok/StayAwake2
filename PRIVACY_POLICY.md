# Privacy Policy — StayAwake

**Last updated: June 2025**

## Overview

StayAwake ("the app") is a driver drowsiness detection tool. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do **not** collect, transmit, or store camera footage or images.
- We do **not** send any biometric data (eye landmarks, EAR values) off your device.
- We do **not** have user accounts or require sign-in.
- We do **not** share any data with third parties for advertising.

## Data Processed On-Device Only

The following is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it:

- **Camera frames** — used in real time to compute eye landmarks. Frames are discarded immediately after processing.
- **Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)** — a numeric metric derived from landmarks, used only to detect drowsiness. Not stored.
- **Calibration baseline** — stored locally in app memory for the duration of a session only.

## Analytics (Local Only)

The app stores anonymous usage events (e.g. "app opened", "alert triggered") in local device storage only. This data:

- Never leaves your device.
- Is used only to show you your own session statistics.
- Can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app.

## Crash Reporting

If you have granted permission, anonymised crash reports may be sent to Sentry (sentry.io) to help us fix bugs. These reports contain:

- Device type and OS version
- App version
- Stack trace of the crash

They do **not** contain camera data, location, or personal identifiers.

## Camera Permission

Camera access is required for live drowsiness detection. If you deny camera permission, the app functions in simulation mode only. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device Settings.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
